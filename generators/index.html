HVAC Micro Energy Harvester

A development concept that harvests a portion of ceiling-HVAC fan rotation to provide low-power energy for wireless IoT sensors.

DEVELOPMENT CONCEPT · UNDER DEVELOPMENT
  1. Ceiling HVAC propeller
  2. Low-loss speed increaser
  3. Low-cogging PCB generator
  4. Power management
  5. Energy storage
  6. Wireless IoT sensor

At low fan speed, a speed increaser may be needed. Generator cogging torque and mechanical resistance can increase fan loading; development focuses on a low-cogging PCB generator and a low-loss transmission.

Goals: battery-free sensor power and compact integration. Sensor examples: temperature, humidity, vibration, air quality and filter condition. Output and the impact on fan loading require validation.
