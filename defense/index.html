APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT

PCB motor & generator technology for defense equipment

Drawing on motor and generator design experience, FluxMotion explores PCB-based machines for the space constraints and operating requirements of defense equipment. Our approach combines electromagnetic design, thermal management and mechanical integration.

BENCHMARKING & TEST ANALYSIS

Generator benchmarking, testing & analysis

FluxMotion undertakes generator benchmarking tests and operating-characteristic analysis alongside generator design. We measure electrical operating conditions and temperature changes at key locations under load, using test data to assess thermal behavior and potential design improvements.

Test setup

Load-bank testing and instrumentation, including field voltage and current checks.

Temperature measurement

Temperature recording at the housing, load winding and field winding.

Time-series data analysis

Temperature-rise trends are compared across measurement locations to examine thermal behavior.
